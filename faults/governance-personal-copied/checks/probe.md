Descriptive only. As `player` on the reporting node, `psql -d <database>`:
`\det+` lists the foreign table over the shop's `customers` on the
second server; `\deu+` its mapping for the loader to the back office's
login; `SELECT jobname, schedule, username FROM cron.job` the job every
thirty minutes as the loader; `SELECT count(*), count(email) FROM <table>`
the copied rows, once the job has run. `~/data-policy.md` on the bastion
says personal data stays in the shop's database.

Expected: names and e-mail addresses in the reporting database, the
foreign table, the server and the job all present.
