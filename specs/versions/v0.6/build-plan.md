## Read first

- **Short tests only** (master spec 12.1, 14): every chunk check, every
  item of a wave gate and every item of the release gate runs **under
  30 minutes**, on the least environment that proves its brick or
  feature, on **one host, armenelos**. The VPS stays drained and
  untouched for the whole build: v0.6 has no two-host run. No aged run,
  no long run: fake aging's full selections with their real tails, the
  blind reads and the stance check-list's v0.6 items are
  [v1.0 gating](../README.md#v10-gating)'s, which already lists them
  (definition of done item 9). A test found to need more than 30
  minutes is cut down or moved to v1.0 gating by a ruling, never run
  long at a gate.
- **No data preflight.** The repository keeps no development data
  register; the data registers here are game content (each scenario's
  handout, generated by the `data-register` skill), and every
  scenario's data is synthetic. There is no `data-steward` step in this
  plan, and chunks carry no **Data** line.
- **No intent register** (no `.atd`, no `intent/`): no `documentalist`
  wave preflight, peek or sync, and chunks carry no **Atoms** or
  **Revisions** line. As in v0.4 and v0.5, the wave driver records each
  wave's capture in the wave log (W1 to W4) before any chunk of the
  wave starts; each chunk's **Architecture capture** line says what its
  wave's capture must fix.
- **A brick bump and its re-pins land in one commit** (v0.5's rule):
  the loader refuses a scenario or fixture pinning a version its
  `brick.yaml` no longer carries, the unit suite loads every scenario,
  and `test_brick_docs.py` requires each brick's "Supported faults" to
  match the faults whose `applies_to` names its version. So chunk 2 owns
  every version line, pin, `applies_to` and those lists; the brick
  chunks (5, 6, 8) own the rest of their brick.
- **The loader refuses no unknown key** (`check_against_schema` reports
  missing keys only): a new key may appear in a file before the chunk
  that checks it. Chunk 3 checks the keys that don't belong to the
  network overview; chunk 7 checks the overview's, and turns on its
  requirements (a scenario listing `domains:` has an overview, its
  customer nodes a `description:`, every domain a `data/overview.yaml`)
  together with the clinic's overview, so the suite stays green.
- **The research branch is read-only.** The prototypes are on
  `research/fake-aging` at `10d1bc6` (`tools/research/fake_aging/`), in
  the locked worktree `~/work/db_audit_gm-wt/research-fake-aging`, with
  the only real-aging reference untracked in its `results/`. No chunk
  writes, cleans, resets or deletes anything there, and nothing is
  cherry-picked from the branch: its commits mix the prototypes with
  run sheets and orchestration that stay research. A chunk reads a
  prototype with `git show 10d1bc6:tools/research/fake_aging/<file>`;
  each chunk's Scope says what it takes and how (rewritten, or copied
  and generalised, naming `10d1bc6` in its commit message). Nothing is
  copied as is (master spec 9.12). **No reference data goes into git**
  (spec D519): chunk 1 archives the whole `results/` folder outside
  git, and a chunk reading the reference extracts what it needs with
  `tools/fake_aging/reference/extract.sh` into an untracked working
  directory; a test needing it skips, saying why, when the archive is
  absent. Lifting the worktree's lock stays
  the owner's, once chunk 1 has merged.
- **The real mode stays as it is.** The scheduler's real scheduling is
  untouched byte for byte; the fill is a separate entry point (master
  spec 16). Chunk 8 adds a replay guard every later scheduler change
  keeps green, and every wave gate reruns the existing scenarios' short
  smokes.
- **Handouts are static** (master spec 7.4): a chunk generating a
  handout (the register, the overview) regenerates it in the same
  commit as the change to any of its inputs, as the loader's digests
  require.
- **ISS-001 closes with chunk 3** (O269's refusal; spec D510), through
  the `issue_management` skill; O251 and O269 are closed in the open
  questions register at wave 1's sync, routed to `spec-writer`.
- **v0.5's lessons, kept**: a new caller waits, bounded, for the names
  it resolves at start (the `pgbouncer` race, spec D305, D356); the
  driver never commits in a wave worktree while a chunk owner may be
  merging into it; a fix-sized finding becomes an inserted chunk, the
  next free integer, its decision first.

## Workflow deltas

- **κ = 2**, the owner's rule: at most two active chunk owners at a
  time, driven from the top-level session. **Docker is serialised**:
  every Docker-using step (image builds, stack tests, throwaway
  containers, fixture deployments, calibration runs) runs under `flock
  /tmp/dbagm-docker.lock <command>`. Each chunk below is marked
  **Docker: heavy**, **light** (one image or a bastion-only fixture,
  minutes) or **none**; the driver pairs a heavy chunk with a light or
  none chunk wherever the wave has one, and when two heavy chunks are
  both active (wave 3's 10 and 11), counts the lock's queue as their
  cost, not a stall.
- **Sleep inhibitor**: every Docker run longer than a few minutes runs
  under `systemd-inhibit --what=sleep:idle:handle-lid-switch`.
- **The VPS is not used**: no placement, no image, no run there; it
  stays a drained worker. **Never `docker * prune`**: superseded
  `dbagm` images are removed by name when the disk runs short.
- **Branches and worktrees**: waves `v0.6-w<n>` in
  `/home/bastien/work/db_audit_gm-wt/v0.6-w<n>`; chunks
  `v0.6-c<id>-<slug>` in
  `/home/bastien/work/db_audit_gm-wt/v0.6-c<id>-<slug>`; the release
  gate `v0.6-rg` in `/home/bastien/work/db_audit_gm-wt/v0.6-rg`. Never
  in the main checkout. Wave 1 is cut from `main` once `v0.6-spec` has
  merged into it (G1).
- **Shared lists**, under the model's in-wave rule:
  `tools/gm/__init__.py`'s verb and option registrations;
  `.claude/skills/game-master/gmctl.md`'s verb table; the root
  `README.md`'s brick and fault tables; the bricks' "Supported faults"
  sections; the smoke scripts' `IMAGES=` lists; each scenario's
  `README.md` when a chunk regenerates it.
- **Chunk checks**, in addition to the model's (v0.5's, kept):
  - Python: `python3 -m unittest discover -s tools/tests -t .`;
  - the scheduler program's unit tests inside its image, `sh
    bricks/scheduler/tests/run-unit-tests.sh`, and the `api` brick's,
    `sh bricks/api/tests/run-unit-tests.sh`, when touched;
  - Python outside the suites (`tools/fake_aging/`, check scripts):
    `python3 -m py_compile` on every file touched;
  - shell: `sh -n` on every shell file touched, those without an
    extension included (s6 `run` files, `rootfs/usr/local/sbin/*`,
    `rootfs/usr/local/lib/gm/*`), and `shellcheck` when installed;
  - YAML: every YAML file touched loads with PyYAML and with the
    loader's duplicate-key refusal;
  - a chunk touching a brick: `docker build -t
    127.0.0.1:5000/dbagm/<brick>:<version> bricks/<brick>` and `sh
    bricks/<brick>/tests/run-stack-test.sh`, under the lock (needs G3,
    G4);
  - **a chunk touching `bricks/scheduler/program/`** (from chunk 8):
    the real mode's replay guard green (chunk 8);
  - **a chunk changing a generated file's input** (a scenario's nodes,
    seeds, domains, purposes, third parties, the answer key): the data
    register and, from chunk 7, the network overview regenerated with
    their skills in the same commit, and the scenario's `README.md`
    with the `scenario-document` skill;
  - **a chunk running a fixture**: `gmctl fit` on it on one host first;
    its time placed, never waited for (a fixture's T0, a job moved, a
    tail capped); the run under 30 minutes with the deployment, its
    figure recorded;
  - a chunk adding or changing a `gmctl` invocation in a Markdown file:
    the invocation parses with `gmctl`'s own parser.
- **Wave gate (full quality command)**, every step under the host lock
  and the sleep inhibitor, on armenelos alone, at κ = 1 for the gate
  itself, **each item under 30 minutes**:
  - the full unit suite; the scheduler's and the `api` brick's unit
    tests; `sh -n` and `py_compile` over every file of their kind;
  - every brick's image build and stack test, one at a time (ten
    bricks), each brick at its v0.6 version from wave 1 on; a rebuild
    whose `dpkg-query` list differs from the version's first build
    folds the change into the unreleased version, with a post-change
    check (a G9 row; spec D445's rule for a released version does not
    apply to an unreleased one);
  - `sh tools/tests/smoke.sh`, `sh tools/tests/smoke-monitoring.sh`,
    `sh tools/tests/smoke-governance.sh armenelos`, `sh
    tools/tests/smoke-load.sh armenelos`;
  - `sh tools/tests/smoke-scenario.sh` on `bookshop-new-server`,
    `fernway-clinics-monitoring`, `tarnbeck-outdoor-shop` and
    `tarnbeck-outdoor-campaign`, their short smoke only (never
    `smoke-purposes.sh`'s `cycles` or `baseline`, v1.0 gating's); the
    clinic is never deployed whole (master spec 14 item 4);
  - every fixture short test the wave's chunks ran, rerun on the merged
    wave branch, one at a time, with its figure;
  - what each wave adds ("Per-wave gate contents").
  A failure is traced to its chunk and fixed on the wave branch in its
  own commit. Then the driver's report to the owner; the merge into
  `main` on the owner's word.
- No link checker is configured; Markdown links are checked by reading.
- Images are tagged `127.0.0.1:5000/dbagm/<brick>:<version>`; every
  pinned download is checked against its published checksum in the
  Dockerfile.
