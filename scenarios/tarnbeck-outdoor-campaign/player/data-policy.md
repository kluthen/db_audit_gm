# Tarnbeck Outdoor: data policy

This policy applies to every application and staff account working with
our customers' data.

## What we hold

- **Personal data**: anything that identifies a customer, or is stored
  with something that does: names, e-mail and postal addresses, country
  and time zone, marketing consent and its history, and the mails and
  declarations we send about them.
- **Customer-linked data**: a customer number with nothing identifying
  beside it, such as the customer on an order or a win-back lead.
- **Internal data**: everything else: products, order totals, figures
  and aggregates.

## Where it may live

Personal and customer-linked data live only in the shop's database
(`shop`, on the shop's database server). They never go into the
reporting database Lumen Analytics runs for us, nor anywhere else.
Internal data may live anywhere.

## Who may read it

- Personal data: the web shop, the back office (it answers customers, so
  it reads names and e-mail addresses), the marketing team's jobs, the
  shipping manager's jobs and the customs gateway's connection.
- Customer-linked data: the web shop, the back office, the marketing
  team's jobs and the shipping manager's jobs.
- Internal data: any of our accounts.

No other account reads personal or customer-linked data.

## What may leave, and to whom

- To our mail provider, through the mail outbox: the recipient's name and
  e-mail address, and for a shipping notice the order number, its
  shipping time, destination country and total; for a campaign mail, the
  campaign.
- To the customs gateway, through the customs outbox: the order
  reference, the consignee's name and address, the destination country,
  the order's lines, their total value and its currency.

Nothing else leaves the company.

## Marketing e-mail

- We send marketing e-mail, such as a win-back campaign, only to
  customers who consented to it. A customer's consent is the latest
  choice they made, granting or withdrawing it, as our consent history
  records it: someone who unsubscribed gets no more marketing e-mail
  from the moment they did.
- Marketing e-mail reaches a customer only between 08:00 and 21:00 in
  the customer's own time zone, wherever they live.
- Shipping notices are not marketing: every customer gets one when their
  order ships, and one only.

## Keeping it

Backups are encrypted. Mails and declarations are kept for as long as
the law requires and no longer.
