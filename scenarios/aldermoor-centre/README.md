## 2. Background

Aldermoor Veterinary Hospitals is a regional franchise of veterinary hospitals. Its centre at Aldermoor runs a small-animal and farm practice with an emergency service, wards, a laboratory that also serves the region's practices, imaging and monitoring machines and an owners' portal. Its record, wards, agenda, portal and back office live on one PostgreSQL instance, records, streamed to a standby in an off-site room; the laboratory and the machines have instances of their own. Integration jobs talk to the franchise's other centres, its head office and messaging service, an external reference laboratory, the authority and an off-site archive.

The story as the players read it: `player/story.md`.

- **Scale**: checked: the core and its five parts at one centre's scale, each as its own fixture measured it: the core about 0.27 million rows, housing 0.14, scheduling 0.13, the lab 0.33, the machinery 0.28, the portal 0.20.
- **Left out** (a fixture's least environment): none: every producer is composed.
- **Merge resolutions**: none: no two sources of one layer collided.
- **Scenario layer**: `compose/backoffice/`, `compose/clinic-api/`, `compose/equipment/`, `compose/interfaces/`, `compose/lis/`, `compose/partners-api/`, `compose/partners-backend/`, `compose/pms/`, `compose/ward/`.
- **Coherence**: checked by `load-data` at every setup, after the loads,
  the derivations and the histories, before any fault is planted; a
  failure is a design error, fixed in the composition or a domain and
  reloaded, never a fault. Its result is setup state, recorded in
  `composition.yaml`'s `coherence:` and the game's `state.yaml`, and not
  shown here.

### `s1`: `purpose-urgent-priority-dropped` on `interfaces`, known

- **What is wrong**: interfaces' post-lab-orders maps the order's priority through {"stat": "urgent"}.get(priority, "routine"), so every centre order reaches the lab routine, the C-{consultation}-urgent ones included.
- **Why it matters**: the lab releases a routine test at the pathologist's next round, an urgent one at once: the emergency team's blood gases and glucose come back hours late (LO4), a round-trip cascade (D286).

Evidence, as the `player` user (and `dba` in psql):

| Where | Source | How | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `node:interfaces` | the transformer's script | `cat /srv/scheduler/repository/scripts/lab_orders.py` | the body's "priority" mapped from "stat" only, "routine" for anything else |
| `bastion` | the lab's orders against the record's requests | `psql -h lab -d lab -c "SELECT id, client_ref, priority FROM lab_orders WHERE account_id = 1 AND client_ref LIKE '%-urgent' ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 10", then the requests of those orders in psql -h records -d clinic` | C-{consultation}-urgent orders routine at the lab, their requests urgent in the record |

Acceptable answers:

- **Diagnosis**: names the mapping in post(): the request's urgent priority sent as routine, the lab holding the tests for its next round; the urgent references routine at the lab as the trace.
- **Proposal**: send the request's own priority; raise the orders already posted routine at the lab, or ask the lab to release them (the backlog).
- **Fix**: post() sends the request's own priority, reloaded: an urgent order posted after the fix reaches the lab urgent and is answered within its turnaround.

### `s2`: `pg-archive-command-silent` on `records`, hidden

- **What is wrong**: records' archive_command is 'vault-post wal %p %f || true': vault-post's name and file swapped, so vault-post refuses every segment's name (pg_wal/...), and `|| true` makes the archiver count each one archived and recycle it; no segment of records has reached the vault since the game's start (lab's archive, to the same vault, still ships).
- **Why it matters**: the WAL archive is what rolls the nightly base backup forward: without it a restore loses up to a day of the clinic's records, and every signal PostgreSQL gives says the archive works.

Evidence, as the `player` user (and `dba` in psql):

| Where | Source | How | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bastion` | pg_stat_archiver on records | `psql -h records -d postgres -c 'SELECT archived_count, last_archived_wal, last_archived_time, failed_count FROM pg_stat_archiver'` | segments archived, failed_count 0 |
| `bastion` | cold storage's manifests (GET /manifests) | `curl -s "http://cold-storage:8000/manifests?type=wal&source=records&from=$(date -u +%F)"` | no manifest names the segments pg_stat_archiver reports archived |
| `bastion` | cold storage's page (GET /) | `curl -s http://cold-storage:8000/ (the wal chart's columns and table)` | no wal shipment on the days since the fault, beside the planted weeks before it (about 30 a day) |
| `node:records` | /etc/postgresql/postgresql.conf and the server log | `grep -n archive_command /etc/postgresql/postgresql.conf; grep -h 'vault-post:' /var/log/postgresql/postgresql-*.log \| tail` | 'vault-post wal %p %f \|\| true', and vault-post refusing the name pg_wal/... once per segment |
